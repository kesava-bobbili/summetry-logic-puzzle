Fill the 3×3 grid with digits 1–9 (repeats allowed). Every row, column, and diagonal must share the same sum. The sum is not shown until you solve it.

Rules

This is a sandbox equal-sum puzzle, not a magic square. Use digits 1–9 in a 3×3 grid. Numbers may repeat. There is no target sum shown while you play.

When every row, every column, and both diagonals is filled, they must all share the same sum. Any valid arrangement counts.

How It Works

  1. Choose Easy (5 clues), Medium (4 clues), or Hard (3 clues).
  2. Fill the empty cells. Clue cells cannot be changed.
  3. When a row, column, or diagonal is fully filled, it is marked green or red immediately.
  4. Green means it matches the current common sum. Red means lines disagree or the sum does not match.
  5. Press Check to validate the whole board.
  6. After you solve it, the common sum you achieved is revealed.

Difficulty

Easy: 5 prefilled clues.

Medium: 4 prefilled clues.

Hard: 3 prefilled clues.

Feedback

Green line — matches the current common sum.

Red line — wrong sum or conflict with other completed lines.
